Who is the primary target for these three website versions?

The old done-deal.info targets "brokers" broadly — let's nail down who we're actually selling to

A

Individual Real Estate Agents

The agent personally doing deals — solo practitioners and small teams. They feel the pain of TC work directly. Messaging: save time, close more deals, never miss a deadline.

B

Brokerage Owners / Team Leads

Buying for 10–800 agents. They care about scale, compliance, and cost reduction. Messaging: replace your TC staff, standardize workflows, scale without headcount.

C

Both — Different messaging per version

One version targets agents, one targets brokerages, one goes broad. Maximum reach, but requires more content variation across the three designs.
